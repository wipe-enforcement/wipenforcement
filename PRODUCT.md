# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are audiovisual rights holders, broadcasters, studios, and their
content-protection, legal, and research teams. They need a reliable way to review
publicly observable offers of works they control and decide what, if anything,
to do next.

## Product Purpose

WIPE is being built as a bilingual measurement and evidence platform for Arabic
and MENA audiovisual content, with a path to broader markets. Its goal is to
help rights holders validate public supply observations and make better
informed enforcement decisions. The owner's initial validation goal is a pilot
reviewed with ten content owners; this is a target, not a current customer or
partner claim.

## Positioning

WIPE focuses on evidence of publicly observable supply, its provenance, and its
relationship to a verified rights calendar. It does not infer a hidden audience
or claim revenue loss from pirate-site traffic. Its intended progression is
measurement, rightsholder review, and—only when authorized—support for
enforcement workflows.

## Operating Context

The first public experience serves rightsholders evaluating the approach. The
owner's intended initial market order is Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates,
Egypt, Kuwait, Lebanon, Iraq, Morocco, then a Levant cluster. This is a rollout
priority, not a market ranking or a measured coverage claim.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The repository contains a Python measurement engine, a static public site,
  evidence and rights-calendar models, and planned collection/publication
  paths.
- No production source feed or live measurement is configured in the
  repository. Public metrics must remain explicitly unmeasured until reviewed
  sources produce a healthy, timestamped snapshot.
- Public presence and authorized collection are separate questions. Each
  source needs a documented purpose, terms and robots review, rate limits,
  retention decision, named operator, and applicable legal review before use.
- Public pages may explain principles and current status, but must not expose
  private watchlists, detection rules, source credentials, or operational
  thresholds.
- English and Arabic are separate, complete authored experiences. Arabic must
  use correct right-to-left layout and language-specific copy, not machine
  translation of English.
- No claim of takedown capability, production readiness, customer validation,
  legal compliance, or measured effectiveness is made unless independently
  verified.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the WIPE Enforcement name. The owner wants an original, futuristic,
mechanical, holographic/wireframe visual identity with purposeful interactivity
and device-responsive behavior. Do not copy Transformers or another
third-party franchise's artwork or branding.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository includes the measurement core, research register, source code,
and tests. The attached project materials describe the intended pilot and
priority countries/services. They do not establish that production collection,
private access, deployment, or a rightsholder pilot is live. No customer logo,
testimonial, paid partnership, or verified global ranking is available for
public use.

## Product Principles

1. A missing observation is unknown, never zero.
2. Keep public supply evidence, authorized audience data, and rights context
   distinct.
3. Give every published measurement a source, method, and collection time.
4. Minimize personal data and keep operational detection intelligence private.
5. Escalate from observation to action through human and rightsholder review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public website must support English and Arabic, correct RTL behavior,
keyboard access, readable contrast, reduced-motion preferences, and responsive
use from mobile through desktop.
