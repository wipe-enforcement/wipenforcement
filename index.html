01 / The approach

Make every number answerable.

WIPE is being built to document observable signals of unauthorised distribution and connect each reported finding to its source, date, and verification trail. The goal is not a bigger number. It is a clearer one—one that a rightsholder can question, reproduce, and correct.

Evidence, not inference

Separate direct observations from estimates. Don’t imply audience size or financial loss where the evidence doesn’t support it.

Traceable by design

Keep source, time, and verification context attached to findings so the method can be examined.

Respect the rights

Use public information responsibly and keep rightsholder review and correction part of the process.

02 / How it works

From observation to accountable record.

A deliberately legible path. Each step should leave enough context for another person to understand what was observed and what it does—and does not—mean.

01 — OBSERVE

Record the signal

Capture relevant public-facing evidence with a clear source and observation time.

02 — VERIFY

Preserve the trail

Check, document, and hash evidence so the record can be reviewed and reproduced.

03 — REPORT

State the limits

Present findings with their scope and uncertainty. Never turn a copy count into an audience claim.

03 / Start with the record

Clarity is a form of respect.
