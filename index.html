A clearer record for Arabic and MENA screen works

Evidence you can replay. Decisions you can defend.

WIPE is being built for rights holders who need a clearer view of publicly observable offers of Arabic and MENA audiovisual works. It connects a source, a rights context, and a reviewable record—without pretending that a listing reveals its audience or financial impact.

Signal path · interactive modelIllustrative · not live data

Begin with a publicly visible offer. An absent observation is not proof that no offer exists.

A signal is a starting point—not a verdict.

The platform is designed to make the distance between a public observation and a responsible decision visible. Each step can be checked, corrected, or declined.

01 / OBSERVATION

Record what is visible

Describe a public offer and its source. Do not infer viewers, intent, or ownership from a single trace.

02 / CONTEXT

Add verified rights context

A work, territory, authorised channel, and licence window need an accountable source.

03 / REVIEW

Invite independent review

Keep uncertainty explicit and give the rightsholder a path to correct or dispute a record.

04 / AUTHORISED ACTION

Support authorised action

Enforcement support is a future, permissioned workflow—not a claim that the site can issue takedowns today.

Designed to grow without hiding uncertainty.

A durable platform should add sources and markets without changing what a metric means. WIPE’s next steps are gated by evidence quality, human review, and operating permission—not by a promise of autonomous growth.

01 / PILOT

Establish a trustworthy pilot

Agree the source, rights scope, review process, and privacy terms with a small number of rightsholders. Publish no live figure until the collection and validation gates pass.

02 / COVERAGE

Expand by proven coverage

Add independent sources market by market; document gaps, failures, retention, and corrections. Keep the public view separate from private monitoring intelligence.

03 / RESILIENCE

Scale with accountable automation

Use replaceable connectors, portable records, tested recovery, and human approval for consequential action. AI may help triage; it does not self-authorise collection, accusation, or enforcement.

Start with one title and one territory.

A focused conversation can define a safe pilot, the evidence a rightsholder needs, and what must remain private.
