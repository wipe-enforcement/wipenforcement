01 · The instrument, live in your browser

Four calculators. Same algebra as the engine. Nothing invented.

These are not mock-ups — they are the platform's actual arithmetic ported to JavaScript: null-contagious estimates, lognormal intervals, bounded shares, per-surface coverage accounting. Where an input is missing, the output refuses. Every refusal here is the instrument working.

Observed audience on the surfaces we can see — e.g. Telegram post view-counters. Log scale; this is a measured input, not an estimate.

The fraction of the pirate population our detectors actually saw — measured by capture–recapture, never assumed. Set it to unknown and watch the quote refuse.

The only assumption in the calculation is a number you supply. Zero means no tariff published → no value quoted.

Calibrated band [10–40%] per market from client ground truth. Never 1:1 — assuming so inflates any loss figure 4×.

Output

The quotation is not a loss figure. It is measured supply × your tariff, arithmetic shown, interval propagated through every input. Markets we cannot price are reported as counts and listed beside the total, never omitted from it.

What fraction of the pirate population one detector sees, assuming each is mutually independent — the honest upper bound.

Real crawlers overlap. Drag it: correlation is why a sixth copy of an existing crawler buys almost nothing, while one orthogonal source buys a lot. This is the procurement question, not an engineering one.

Output

The 95% figure in the industry brief is a fleet-architecture decision with an integer answer, not an aspiration. The planner prints it.

The production path consumes feeds we do not control — certificate transparency and the redemption drop list — and asks of each arriving name only: does the grammar explain it? Type or edit any name.

Classification

Runs on the public example corpus (three invented brands). The real target set is private intel, deliberately never published: a machine-readable watch list tells operators exactly which patterns are monitored, and every pattern not on it becomes free. The machinery is identical; only the corpus is withheld.

Classification & response

Severity comes from the copy's relationship to the rights calendar, never from its size. A live final with 500 simultaneous viewers outranks a 3-year-old series with 5 million — and a pre-release leak is the only class with a defendant the network already knows by name.

02 · What the research found

The supply side publishes its own search method — and its own rotation

25 cited source facts, registered with provenance in docs/SOURCES.md. Three structural findings changed the architecture of the platform.

SEOthe method, verbatim

Pirate networks bid on each other's names. Searching like an expert works because it is reverse-engineered supply-side advertising.

نمط عناوين البحث الشائعة: اسم الفيلم + السنة + «مدبلج» + «HD أون لاين» + أسماء مواقع المشاهدة للظهور في نتائج البحث

"The common search-title pattern: film name + year + 'dubbed' + 'HD online' + the names of rival sites, in order to appear in search results."

One of the region's largest pirate networks, in its own page metadata · cited source #15

Brand co-occurrence in one meta block is therefore attribution, not accident — a lookalike domain was recorded carrying seven rival brands at once. And the quality tag is a confession: WEB-DL names a licensee's platform as the source; TS/CAM names a physical cinema. Dubbed vs subtitled is a demographic read on where the operator thinks the money is — obtained without asking a single viewer.

CT logsthe blind spot, closed

Subdomain rotation produces no WHOIS event at all — certificate transparency is the only surface that sees it.

Rotation is not only registering a new domain; it is w<n>.<brand>.<tld> → w<n+1>, and the apex is never touched, so a registration monitor is structurally blind to the corpus's most mechanical move. But an operator cannot serve TLS without a certificate for each name — and the name appears in the log before the site serves anything. The recognition layer therefore consumes CT and the redemption drop list, and classifies what arrives. It never generates target lists: watching hypothetical names is watching mostly things that don't exist, and a public watch list is an evasion manual.

1 → 100the supplier, not the site

Separate brand families run one identical application route. The enforcement target is the vendor.

Hosts of two unrelated brand families were recorded serving the same script, the same parameter, the same fixed-width hex id — the piracy-as-a-service layer the MPA documents by name ("off-the-shelf streaming operations"). One notice to a supplier removes a hundred sites; one notice to a site removes one. Route signatures are kept strictly separate from operator attribution: a shared vendor must never be allowed to look like a shared owner. And the app layer holds the fastest path in the system: impersonating a legal platform's mark is removable under store policy — no infringement proof, no jurisdiction, no takedown.

04 · Method & evidence discipline

Every number carries a receipt, a grade, and the reason it might be wrong

Evidence grades — uncertainty is priced per grade

GradeMeaning±95%
E0Hunch. Never leaves the building.—
E1Signal (autocomplete, ranking move)×0.17–5.8
E2Observed: page read, hashed, timestamped×0.50–2.0
E3Archived: + immutable copy, external time stamp×0.68–1.5
E4Notarised: + third-party attestation×0.79–1.3

Arithmetic takes the weakest link: a product of an E4 and an E1 is an E1. Values are medians, never means — pirate audiences are skewed, and a mean is dragged up by exactly the outliers that flatter a loss figure.

What the instrument refuses

  • —No revenue-loss figure. No counterfactual could support one.
  • —No accusation on one signal, a shared template, or a shared CDN.
  • —No extrapolation without measured coverage. A missing divisor is never 1.0.
  • —No number without a replayable receipt. No recipe → log line, not evidence.
  • —No pack issued from a tampered receipt; the whole pack is refused.
  • —No media stored. No viewer identified. No learned policy that can't be explained to a regulator.

34 of the engine's 77 contract checks are these refusals, and the count is measured at runtime, not typed into a summary.

Chain of custody

Capture → two hashes (bytes + tag skeleton) + replay recipe (resolver, TLS chain, egress, header order) → sealed receipt → append-only Merkle chain, deletion-detecting → daily root anchored to an external time authority → evidence pack. An unanchored day says so on the first line of any pack built from it — implying a protection the system does not have is worse than admitting the gap. Standard library only, deterministic, re-runnable by people who do not trust us: an estimator nobody can re-run is not evidence, it is a receipt from a vendor.

06 · For broadcasters, studios and rights holders

Ask for the pre-release set first. It is the only finding with a name attached.

What you can act on Monday

A copy circulating before you broadcast it came from a screener, a feed, or a post-production drive. Traceable to a partner you already named, without any cooperation from a pirate, a host, or a foreign authority. Volume-ranked dashboards bury this under archive copies.

What the quotation is

Your tariff × our measured count, arithmetic shown, interval propagated, unpriced markets listed beside the total. The only assumption in it is a number you supplied — which is what makes it survive the meeting instead of starting one.

Why share your ground truth

First-party availability and viewership data (NDA, calibration only, never published raw) sharpens the rights calendar for every other participant in your territory. The data network effect runs through accuracy, not volume.
