Twenty years of anti-piracy reporting has produced audience estimates nobody can disprove — which is the same as nobody believing. Demand is uncountable. Supply is enumerable. Every unauthorised offering has to be public somewhere in order to earn anything, so we measure that, and we never ask a single question about what a viewer would have paid.

The industry asks

How many people watched illegally?

Requires assuming a counterfactual no one can observe. Published “loss” figures differ by 4× purely because of where each vendor sets that dial. Unfalsifiable — therefore worthless, and known to be.

WIPE asks

Where is it offered, who runs it, and what does it earn?

Every term is observable, countable, and checkable by a hostile third party from our receipts alone. A claim that can be broken is a claim you can win.

02 · WHY SEARCHING WORKS

The supply side publishes its own search method

We did not infer how Arabic pirate content is found. One of the largest networks in the region describes its own titling convention, in its own meta description, and the reason is commercial: it bids on its rivals.

نمط عناوين البحث الشائعة: اسم الفيلم + السنة + «مدبلج» + «HD أون لاين» + أسماء مواقع المشاهدة (ماي سيما، شاهد فور يو، عرب سيد) للظهور في نتائج البحث

“The common search-title pattern: film name + year + ‘dubbed’ + ‘HD online’ + the names of rival sites (MyCima, Shahid4U, ArabSeed), in order to appear in search results.”

Laroza network, page meta description · larroza.homes / larroza.quest · 2026

An expert searcher finds more by querying the same way, for exactly that reason: they are not searching for the work, they are searching the way the trade advertises it. Which means the technique is also a reading of the operator — and it has two applications the practitioners never intended.

Brand stacking is attributionbrand_signal()

A lookalike eegebest.com was found carrying keyword metadata for the entire family — egybest, mycima, wecima, cima4u, arabseed, akwam, faselhd. Nobody who owns one site bids on six competitors. Whoever registers rotation stock in bulk advertises for all of it. One mention is a mention; three or more in a single meta block is rotation stock.

Dub vs sub is a market readlanguage_mode

Dubbing is expensive and aims at Gulf and Egyptian audiences. Subtitling is cheap and aims at the Levant and Maghreb. Which one an operator invests in tells you where they think the money is — a demographic signal obtained without asking a single viewer, which is the only demographic data we trust.

Quality tags name the leak sourceprovenance

WEB-DL means an extract from a legal platform — a supply-chain fact about a licensee. TS/CAM means a camera in a physical cinema. SCREENER means a review copy. Different crimes, different defendants, and the pirate prints which one it was, in the filename, to advertise quality to its own customers.

Arabic writes the tech in Arabic scriptarabic_technical_terms()

اتش دي is HD. بلوراي is BluRay. بوكس اوفيس is box office. دي في دي is DVD. A parser looking for the Arabic translation of these words finds nothing, because nobody uses it — and generic Arabic NLP misses this stratum entirely. Titles are also bidirectional, so a monolingual generator misses the mixed-script layer too.

03 · THE PART THAT CHANGES THE TIMING

Rotation stock is bought in advance, so the next domain is predictable

Egypt shut MyCima down. It returned within days as WeCima on wecima.tube — and the weciima.* replacements had been registered before the rebrand. Vowel doubling plus a fixed set of cheap new gTLDs is a grammar, and a grammar enumerates. We induced the rules from the observed pairs, then tested the generator against the record:

5 of 5 of the documented MyCima stock, from the brand name and nothing else. So a domain that is registered, in that TLD set, carrying a known brand token, and resolving to nothing yet is a scheduled appearance. Watching the WHOIS/RMRD new-domain feed for that set gives notice in days, before the traffic moves — the only moment a network can be interrupted rather than merely observed. Everything else in this industry watches the past.

The registration feed is not enoughcertificate_transparency

We found the hole in our own method while researching it. Rotation is not only registering a new domain — it is w12.mycima.movie → w13.mycima.movie, and that produces no WHOIS event whatsoever, because the apex domain is never touched. A registration monitor is structurally blind to the most common rotation in the corpus. Certificate transparency is the only surface that sees it: an operator cannot serve TLS without a certificate for each name, and the name appears in the log before the site serves anything. So we watch the certificates, and the drop list besides — a brand-adjacent name reaching redemption is a name about to change hands.

One route links brands that look unrelatedroute_of()

Four hosts, two separate brand families, one application: w12.mycima.movie/watch.php?vid=…, larozza.living/video.php?vid=…, larroza.baby, larroza.homes — same script names, same parameter, same fixed-width hex id. That is not one owner; it is the piracy-as-a-service layer the MPA documents directly (“off-the-shelf streaming operations”: Vidsrc, WHMCS Smarters, HydraHD). Which makes the enforcement target the supplier: one notice to a vendor removes a hundred sites, where one notice to a site removes one. We keep this separate from operator attribution on purpose — a shared vendor must never be allowed to look like a shared owner.

The app store is the fastest pathparse_release()

This corpus names its products after the platforms it pirates: SHAHID PLUS, ALIFLIX, MTNTV, BLADEUHD, VICTV, CRYSTAL OTT, STORM ONE, ATLAS PR0. Impersonating a registered mark is actionable in a store on its own merits — no proof of any act of infringement, no foreign jurisdiction, no takedown. Besides which, an Android package id is the reversed domain the developer controlled, and it is essentially never rotated, because changing it breaks every install: com.aflamtvmaroc.moviesdarija leaks aflamtvmaroc.com, which may have expired years ago. A version sequence (V4.1 → V4.1a → V10.0 → V11.0.0) ties a rebrand to the same developer across a full domain, package and template rotation — which no infrastructure signal survives.

Succession is a public eventlineage_edge()

WeCima marketed itself as بديل ماي سيما — the alternative to MyCima — and absorbed EgyBest’s audience while EgyBest was down. A platform that counts brands counts increases; one that tracks lineage counts one operator continuing. It is the strongest operator finding available without anybody’s cooperation, because the successor advertises the inheritance.

05 · WHAT ACTUALLY RUNS

Four instruments, none of which models a viewer

Rights calendarmodel.py

Severity comes from a copy’s relationship to your licence, never from its size. A live final with 500 simultaneous viewers outranks a three-year-old series with 5 million, because the first is substitution at a rate near one inside a window closing in minutes. PRE_RELEASE_LEAK is the only class with a defendant you already know by name — a screener, a feed, a post-production drive — traceable without anybody’s cooperation. NEVER_AUTHORISED is reported as an unserved market, not as theft.

Receiptreceipt.py

A capture that is born admissible or it is a log line. The facts that make a capture re-verifiable in two years — which resolver answered, which TLS certificate was presented, the exact request bytes — cannot be reconstructed later, so they are recorded at the moment of capture. Two hashes: one pins the bytes, one pins the tag skeleton, which is stable across the one-copyright-year difference between a mirror and its original — so the same number that detects tampering also identifies template clones, free.

Operator graphoperator.py

Titles do not go to court; operators do. Sources are clustered on shared infrastructure, weighted by inverse document frequency, so a Cloudflare IP scores approximately zero automatically — no blocklist for the next provider to evade by being new. Two hard floors sit on top, because a weighted sum can always be coaxed over a line by accumulating near-nothings. A favicon and a template are things you can buy from the same catalogue of dozens, so they corroborate and never decide. Below the floors the word is CO_HOSTED, not OPERATOR — and the distance between those two words is the distance between a lead and a libel.

Evidence ledgerledger.py

A hash chain proves one thing did not change. It does not prove something was not omitted — and omission is the real attack: prune an inconvenient week and every remaining seal stays valid. So entries commit to their predecessor, days fold into Merkle roots for external anchoring, and an unanchored day says so on the first line of any pack built from it. Implying a protection the system does not have is worse than admitting the gap.

Reappearance latencymetrics.py

The most valuable single number here, and it needs no model of human behaviour at all: a duration between two observed events, reproducible by any third party from the receipts. Removal → re-detection measures the operator, not the page. Absence is observed, never taken from a reply — waiting for a notice to be acknowledged is waiting for an adversary to answer, and 66% of the time they will not, so non-response becomes a dated IGNORED outcome rather than a blank cell. And the mirror hop (removed here, back there, inside the window) is the empirical refutation of per-URL enforcement.

06 · VERIFICATION

The instrument declines, and it can be re-run by people who do not trust us

Standard library only. No PyMC, no ClickHouse, no Neo4j, no Kafka — whatever an enterprise architecture would prefer. Not from thrift, but because an estimator nobody can re-run on their own machine is not evidence, it is a receipt from a vendor. Deterministic, no network at test time, milliseconds on a free runner. Hierarchical Bayesian pooling is replaced by method-of-moments empirical Bayes, re-derivable by hand from one printed formula.

178 unit tests 82 contract checks 35 of them refusals 15 contract sections exit 0 0 fabricated zeros 44 cited sources 17 surfaces

The refusal count is measured at runtime rather than typed into a summary, because a headline figure that was not observed would break the rule this design exists to enforce — and a report’s own summary line is the easiest place in the system for that kind of error to hide unnoticed.

  • Never a revenue-loss figure — no counterfactual could support it
  • Never an accusation on one signal, a template, or a shared CDN
  • Never a number without a receipt, a date and a hash
  • Never extrapolation without measured coverage — a missing divisor is not 1.0
  • No media stored: metadata, fingerprints and hashes only
  • No learned collection policy — what adapts is where to look, printed as a sentence
  • No individual viewers identified, ever
  • Never a probe count read as a coverage figure — 110 queries on one surface is one detector

For broadcasters, studios and rights holders

We can produce, per title and per territory: what is being offered, on how many sources, by how many distinct operations, with what reappearance latency after removal — and which instalments appeared before you broadcast them.

The last of those is the one to ask for first. It is the only class of infringement with a defendant you already know by name, and it is measurable today without any cooperation from a pirate, a host or a foreign authority.
