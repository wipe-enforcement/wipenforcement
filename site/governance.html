Privacy and governance

A public site is not permission to collect.

The legal and privacy position depends on the source, data, purpose, jurisdiction, and deployment.

This website

This static site does not provide a login, accept files, or publish a live collection feed. It stores the chosen language in the browser so the site can remember English or Arabic. No analytics or external font service is included in this implementation.

Before a source is used

Review the source’s terms, robots directives, access controls, permitted rate, personal-data exposure, purpose, retention, and applicable local law.

Before a finding is shared

Minimise data, separate public observations from rightsholder-supplied records, restrict access by role, preserve correction history, and avoid exposing private monitoring operations.

Local review is required

Qualified local counsel must review applicable privacy, copyright, evidence, cross-border transfer, and intermediary rules before collection or evidence use.
