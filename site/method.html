How the evidence is meant to work

Make each claim smaller, clearer, and easier to check.

WIPE separates what was observed from what is inferred.

Observe, with permission and limits

A source is assessed before use for purpose, terms, robots guidance, rate limits, privacy impact, retention, and an accountable operator.

Keep provenance with the record

A measurement should state what it counts, where and when it was collected, the method used, its coverage, and known limitations.

Separate observation from rights conclusions

A public offer may warrant review, but it does not by itself prove infringement, ownership, intent, damages, or a particular legal remedy.

Protect operational intelligence

Public pages explain the evidence standard, not private watchlists, detection patterns, source credentials, or collection thresholds.

Define what one observation means

A count should name its unit—for example, one work, service, country, and observation window—and explain how duplicate listings are treated.

Show coverage beside the count

State which public surfaces, languages, queries, and dates were checked, as well as gaps, failed checks, and markets not observed. A detected count is not a complete universe.

Make correction part of the record

A review route should cover mistaken matches, licensed use, and territory or window differences. Preserve corrections and unresolved disputes alongside confirmed observations.

Unknown is a valid result

No feed, stale data, weak coverage, conflicting rights records, or missing provenance must never be rendered as zero, a clean bill of health, or a confident accusation.

These are product principles, not a legal opinion or a claim that production controls are already deployed.
